实用职场短语Useful Workplace Phrases
Say these — politely and confidently大方礼貌地说出来
情境Situation 英语（说出来）English (say this) 中文Chinese
不懂任务Don't understand a task "Could you show me how?" 您可以示范给我看吗？Can you show me how?
需要重复Need a repeat "Could you say that again, please?" 请再说一遍好吗？Please say that again.
说太快Too fast "Could you speak a little slower?" 请说慢一点好吗？Please speak more slowly.
礼貌拒绝Politely decline "I'd like to help, but I can't today. Could I do it tomorrow?" 我想帮忙，但今天不行。可以明天吗？I'd like to help, but not today. Tomorrow?
主动找事做Ask for more work "I finished. What should I work on next?" 我做完了。接下来做什么？I'm done. What's next?
请假Time off "May I take next Friday off? I have a family appointment." 下周五我可以请假吗？我有家事。May I take Friday off? Family matter.
病假Calling in sick "Hi, this is [Name]. I'm not feeling well today, I can't come in." 您好，我是 [姓名]。今天身体不舒服，不能上班。Hi, [Name]. Sick today, can't come in.

表达不同意Disagreement 含蓄、给面子Indirect, save face 直接但礼貌Direct but polite
